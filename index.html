We make the assets agencies ship, and the tools creators keep.

What we produce

Motion & content assets

Finished deliverables for campaigns and channels: animated graphics, 3D renders, edited video, and design assets sized and versioned for wherever they run.

  • Motion graphics & animation (After Effects, Cinema 4D)
  • 3D modeling, lookdev, and rendering
  • Video editing & color (DaVinci Resolve)
  • Generative-AI-assisted stills and video, art-directed from previz
How we work

As a subcontractor to agencies

We plug into an agency's existing pipeline as a white-label production partner. The agency owns the client relationship; we own the deliverable, the deadline, and the file hygiene.

  • Fixed-scope, milestone-billed engagements
  • Layered, organized source files handed over with every delivery
  • Naming, versioning, and spec conventions documented per project
  • Currently in production on agency campaign assets (2026)

About

A small company, on purpose.

Abstract Notion was founded by Hector J. Ortiz, a motion and 3D artist who spent years building the same project scaffolding by hand on every job. The studio pays for the tools; the tools come from what the studio learns.

We keep the company deliberately small so the person you email is the person doing the work.
