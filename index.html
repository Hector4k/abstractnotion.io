Products

Know how a project is built without opening it.

In development · For After Effects

Project Intelligence

A companion app for Adobe After Effects that captures how a project is built — its comments, structure, assets, fonts, colors, and thumbnails — and keeps that context readable by anyone, inside or outside After Effects.

Every project you've worked on becomes a record you can hand off, revisit, or archive with confidence, instead of a heavy file only its author understands.

macOS & Windows · Private testing · projectintelligence.io

projectintelligence.io
Comments, promoted

Notes that survive the project

After Effects lets you comment on any layer or item, but nothing ever surfaces those comments. In a project with hundreds of layers, a note only helps if you already know where it is. Project Intelligence lists every comment in the project in one place — inside After Effects or out — and lets you organize and tag them, so you can leave a crumb trail for your future self or the next artist.

Handoff & archive

Diagnose in minutes, not days

Mark which comps are the render comps and which ones matter, and write notes alongside them about how the project is wired up and how the compositions relate. Whoever opens it next — another artist, or you in three years — gets a map of the build before opening a single comp, instead of days spent reverse-engineering it.

Visual record

A flipbook of every project

Project Intelligence captures thumbnails, not just metadata — for every composition, or every asset in the project, your choice. Each one gets a short sequence of frames rather than a single still, so you can recognize a comp at a glance without rendering or opening anything.

Offline library

Answers without chasing the artist

Every project is indexed into a library the whole team can search offline. Artists, leads, and producers can look up which fonts, colors, logo version, and file paths a past project used — without launching After Effects or waiting on whoever built it.
