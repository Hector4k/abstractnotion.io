Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

This policy explains how Abstract Notion Inc. ("Abstract Notion," "we," "us") handles information when you visit abstractnotion.io or contact us. It does not cover separate products or services that come with their own privacy terms.

What we collect

  • Information you send us. When you email or call us, we receive what you choose to share — typically your name, email address, company, phone number, and details about your project.
  • Technical data. This website has no sign-up forms, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking cookies of our own. Like any website, the servers that deliver it record basic request data such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested.

Service providers

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), and its typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts (Google LLC). These providers receive your IP address and browser information when your browser requests their files, and handle that data under their own privacy policies. Our email runs on Google Workspace.

How we use information

  • To reply to inquiries, prepare quotes, and deliver work you engage us for
  • To manage early-access lists for our software, when you ask to join
  • To keep business records and meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Retention

We keep correspondence and project records for as long as needed for the purposes above and for our legal and accounting obligations, then delete them.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, or to remove you from an early-access list, by emailing hector@abstractnotion.io. We will respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is intended for businesses and professionals and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Software products

Project Intelligence and any other software we publish will include its own privacy notice describing what the software collects, if anything, before you install or use it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
