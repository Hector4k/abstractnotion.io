Process

How a project moves.

  1. Brief & scopeSend the brief, deadline, and deliverable specs. You get scope, schedule, and a quote within one business day.
  2. PrevizBoards, styleframes, or animatics to lock the look and timing before full production spend.
  3. ProductionThe right crew is assembled for the job and scaled to it, with regular reviews against the schedule.
  4. Delivery & handoffFinal files to spec, plus organized, documented source projects so the next team can pick them up.
