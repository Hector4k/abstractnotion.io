About

Experience on one side, tools on the other.

The company

Abstract Notion was founded by Hector J. Ortiz after twenty-five years in motion design, visual effects, and themed entertainment — directing, supervising VFX, and producing for brands, agencies, and theme parks around the world.

The studio takes on production work where that experience makes a difference. The products side builds the software that experience showed was missing, starting with Project Intelligence for After Effects.

Why software

Years of inheriting other people's project files — and handing off our own — made one gap obvious: the knowledge of how a project is built rarely survives the person who built it. Project Intelligence exists to close that gap for motion artists and the producers who depend on them.

The founder

Hector J. Ortiz is a director, VFX supervisor, producer, and artist whose work spans national commercial campaigns, projection mapping and LED media for theme parks and live shows, color grading and HDR finishing, and 3D. He builds pipeline tools for the same work, in Python, ExtendScript, and TypeScript.
